# 📊 Power BI Portfolio

Welcome to my **Power BI Portfolio**, where I document data analytics projects built using Power BI. Each project lives in its own subfolder below.

## 📁 Projects

### [💳 Consumer Financial Complaints](https://github.com/thisisChloe/PowerBI/tree/main/Consumer%20Financial%20Complaints)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that looks at consumer financial complaints: what people complain about, which companies they complain about, and how well those complaints get resolved.

### [🛒 E-Commerce & SaaS Revenue Analytics Platform](https://github.com/thisisChloe/PowerBI/tree/main/E-Commerce%20%26%20SaaS%20Revenue%20Analytics%20Platform)

An interactive Power BI report that looks at how a software business makes its money: which plans and deals drive revenue, how loyal customers are, and where money is being lost through refunds and promo codes.

### [📦 Business Performance & Supply Chain Dashboard](https://github.com/thisisChloe/PowerBI/tree/main/Business%20Performance%20%26%20Supply%20Chain%20Dashboard)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that tracks how a retail business is selling, who it's selling to, and how well it gets orders to customers, bringing sales, customers and supply chain together in one report.
